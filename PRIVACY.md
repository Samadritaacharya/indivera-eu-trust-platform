# IndiVera privacy-by-design notes

The beta minimizes personal data and is built for business/product evidence, not consumer profiling.

- Do not upload passports, national IDs, bank statements, health data or employee records.
- Collect only business/product evidence needed for readiness, verification and matching.
- Keep public buyer-facing data separate from private evidence.
- Do not train models on uploaded supplier content by default.
- Do not send documents to third-party AI without explicit configuration and processor/data-location review.
- Add deletion, retention, access-control and export workflows before production onboarding.
- Log decisions and metadata, not credentials or unnecessary raw sensitive content.
