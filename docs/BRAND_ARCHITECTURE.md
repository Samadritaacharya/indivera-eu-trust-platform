# IndiVera brand architecture

## Umbrella

**IndiVera** — *Indian supply. European confidence.*

## Products

- **IndiVera Ready** — market readiness and evidence gaps. *Evidence before export.*
- **IndiVera Verified** — supplier and evidence review. *Evidence behind every supplier.*
- **IndiVera Match** — buyer/supplier matching. *Find India without the sourcing guesswork.*
- **IndiVera Passport** — traceability and provenance record. *Know where it came from. Know what backs it.*
- **IndiVera Trade** — RFQ/procurement orchestration.

## Visual language

Charcoal, warm ivory, deep indigo and muted terracotta/copper. Avoid flags touching, Taj Mahal/elephant/mandala/paisley clichés and generic certification checkmarks. India is expressed through verified origin, maker stories, materials and craftsmanship.

## Terminology guardrail

`IndiVera Verified` means a platform evidence-review state. UI and documentation must never visually or verbally imply an official EU/Indian government standard, conformity assessment or certification.
